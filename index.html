THE PROJECT

Tax Wise brings together intelligent reasoning, explainable tax information, advisory language, and predictive recommendations in one research-driven decision support system.

THE RESEARCH DOMAIN

Turning tax complexity
into understanding.

A modular AI-driven approach to help individuals navigate Sri Lankan income tax information and explore personalized, evidence-informed guidance.

01 / PROBLEM
⌁

Tax information can be difficult to navigate.

Tax rules, amendments, calculations, and individual circumstances can make it challenging to understand obligations and available options.

02 / RESEARCH GAP
◌

Answers need context and evidence.

Useful tax decision support should connect recommendations and explanations to relevant rules, taxpayer details, and traceable sources.

03 / APPROACH
✳

One coordinated system.

Tax Wise combines four complementary components to support semantic reasoning, explainable optimization, tax advisory, and predictive recommendations.

RESEARCH OBJECTIVES

Designed around better-informed decisions.

The project explores how AI techniques can support personalized income tax compliance and advisory in the Sri Lankan context.

01

Interpret transaction and taxpayer information in a meaningful context.

02

Support tax strategy analysis with explanations linked to relevant tax evidence.

03

Provide an accessible advisory interaction for tax-related questions.

04

Explore personalized recommendations and potential impacts using predictive modelling.
